Guava-Bean "Raita Royale"

Sweet Base • Spicy Roof • Savory Core
⏱️ 30 Mins 🌶️ High Spice 🏆 Mum Approved

A daring fusion of North Indian "Chatpata" flavors and rustic textures. This recipe balances the floral sweetness of guava with the earthy depth of beans, all suspended in a sweetened yogurt base to fight the heat of six green chilies.

The Build (Ingredients)

The "Sweet" Base

  • Dahi: Plain Curd (User's Choice)
  • Milk: 1 Cup (for consistency)
  • Sweetener: Khaand (Raw Sugar)
  • Seasoning: Black Salt, Black Pepper, Bhuna Jeera

The "Spicy" Fruit

  • Guavas: 3 Total (2 firm, 1 ripe)
  • Heat: 6 Green Chilies (No red!)
  • Tadka: Mustard Oil, Jeera
  • Seasoning: White Table Salt

The "Savory" Core

  • Veg: 1 Onion, Small bowl of Green Beans
  • Secret Weapon: 1 Sachet Tomato Ketchup
  • Masala: Catch Masala, Saunf, Ajwain, Hing
  • Tadka: Mustard Oil, Rai (Mustard Seeds)

The Algorithm (Instructions)

Compile the Guava Reduction Blend guavas (seeds included) with green chilies. Heat mustard oil in a pan, crackle the Jeera, and pour in the pulp. Add white salt. Simmer until reduced and concentrated. Set aside to cool.
Render the Savory Paste Blend the dark red onion and beans (no chilies). In a separate pan, heat mustard oil with Rai and Hing. Add the paste. Stir in Saunf powder, Catch Masala, Ajwain, and the Ketchup Sachet. Cover and cook ("let it go brrr") until roasted.
Initialize the Base In a large bowl, whisk the Plain Dahi with 1 cup of milk. Add the Khaand (crucial for heat balance), Bhuna Jeera, Black Pepper, and Black Salt. Whisk until silky.
Deployment (Assembly) 1. Fold the slightly cooled Onion-Bean paste into the sweet curd mix.
2. Temper the mix by adding the hot Guava Reduction one spoon at a time.
3. Adjust salt (add more Black Salt to taste).
Final Commit Serve immediately with a massive "flood" of Masala Boondi on top for crunch.
